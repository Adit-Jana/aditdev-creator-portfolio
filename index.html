How an AI reads your question.

  1. You type a question.To you, it's one sentence.
  2. It's cut into tokens.Small chunks of text. The model only ever sees these.
  3. Each token looks back at the others.That's attention: the model weighs which earlier words matter.
  4. It writes the answer one token at a time.Every new word is a prediction. That's all a reply is.
