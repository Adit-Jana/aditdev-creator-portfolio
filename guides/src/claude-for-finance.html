01The rules first

Read thisThis guide is education, not financial or investment advice. I am not a licensed adviser. Claude can make mistakes with numbers and does not see live market prices unless you connect a data source. Check every figure before you act on it.
  • Never give Claude your bank, broker or UPI passwords, PINs or OTPs.
  • Never let an AI place trades or move money for you.
  • Remove account numbers and your name from statements before you upload them.
  • Ask Claude to show its working: formulas, sources and assumptions.
  • Use Claude to understand and organise. Make the decision yourself.

02A budget from your bank statement

Download last month's statement as CSV or PDF from your bank's app. Remove personal details. Then give it to Claude with this prompt.

Here is my bank statement for October (CSV). Do this:
1. Put every transaction into one category: rent, groceries, food delivery,
   transport, bills, subscriptions, shopping, EMI, investments, other.
2. Show a table: category, total, % of spending, number of transactions.
3. List every subscription and repeated payment with its monthly cost.
4. Show my 5 biggest "small leaks" (many small payments that add up).
5. Suggest a 50/30/20 budget for next month based on my real income,
   with one specific cut in each overspent category.
Show your working. Don't guess at transactions you can't read; list them.
Do it monthlyKeep each month's output. After three months ask: "Compare these three months. What changed and what is a trend?"

04Your trading journal, analysed

Most traders don't lose because of one bad trade. They lose to the same habit, repeated. Export your trade history from your broker as CSV and ask Claude to find it.

Here is my trade history (CSV: date, symbol, side, qty, entry, exit, fees).
Analyse my behaviour, not the market:
1. Win rate, average win, average loss, profit factor, and net P&L after fees.
2. Results by weekday, by hour of entry, and by holding time.
3. Do I hold losers longer than winners? Show the numbers.
4. Trades taken within 30 minutes after a loss (revenge trading?) and how
   they performed.
5. My 3 most costly habits, each with the evidence.
Do not tell me what to buy or sell. Only describe my patterns.

Builds an evidence-backed fundamental report on one listed company (US, global, NSE or BSE): verified data, ratios, a peer comparison and a scorecard.

npx skills add alirezarezvani/claude-skills --skill stock-analysis
Use stock-analysis on [company, NSE: SYMBOL]. Show revenue, profit,
margins, debt and cash flow for 5 years, compare with 3 listed peers, and
list the 3 biggest risks. Cite where each number came from. Mark anything
you could not verify. No buy or sell recommendation.

10Master prompts

Your money in one page

Act as a patient personal-finance teacher, not an adviser. From the
statements I paste, make a one-page summary of my finances: monthly income,
fixed costs, flexible spending, savings rate, emergency fund in months,
and total EMI as % of income. Then explain in plain words what each number
means and what a healthy range usually looks like. Ask me before assuming.

Learn before you invest

I'm a beginner investor in India. Teach me, one concept per message, with
an example in rupees: compounding, SIP vs lump sum, index funds vs active
funds, expense ratio, asset allocation, and why an emergency fund comes
first. After each concept, ask me one question to check I understood.
